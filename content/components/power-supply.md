Everything between the grid and the server plug: grid connection, substation, transformers, UPS, batteries and backup generators.

**Zoom level 2** · **Parts:** Grid interconnection, on-site substation, transformers, switchgear, UPS and batteries, diesel or gas backup generators, power distribution units

## Impacts

| Impact | What happens | Branch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Operational electricity | Most of the facility's environmental footprint runs through this path | [[branches/engineering]] |
| Grid carbon | Emissions depend on the regional grid mix and any on-site generation | [[branches/environment]] |
| Indirect water | Power plants use water; most of a data center's water footprint can be indirect | [[branches/environment]] |

## Sourced facts

- US data centers used about 176 TWh in 2023, with scenarios of 325–580 TWh by 2028 ([LBNL 2024](https://eta-publications.lbl.gov/publications/2024-lbnl-data-center-energy-usage-report)).
- About three quarters of US data centers' operational water footprint was indirect, from electricity generation ([Siddik, Shehabi & Marston 2021](https://eta-publications.lbl.gov/publications/environmental-footprint-data-centers); summary in [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/analysis/data-center-water-usage-remains-hidden/)).
