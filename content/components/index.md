The garden zooms from the whole site down to the materials. Each part links to the impacts it drives. The [building explorer](../static/explorer.htm) shows the same parts as a picture.

| Zoom | Component | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [[components/site-and-land\|Site and land]] | The plot, its location, and everything the location decides: grid, water, climate, neighbors and rules. |
| 2 | [[components/building-shell\|Building shell]] | The structure that holds everything: foundations, walls, roof, raised floors and fit-out. |
| 2 | [[components/power-supply\|Power supply]] | Everything between the grid and the server plug: grid connection, substation, transformers, UPS, batteries and backup generators. |
| 2 | [[components/cooling\|Cooling]] | Moves heat from chips to the outside air or water: chillers, cooling towers, air handlers, and liquid loops. |
| 3 | [[components/white-space\|Data hall and racks]] | The 'white space' where IT equipment lives: rows of racks, aisles, power distribution and cabling. |
| 4 | [[components/servers\|Servers]] | The computers themselves, and how long they stay in service before being replaced. |
| 5 | [[components/chips-and-boards\|Chips and boards]] | GPUs, CPUs, memory and circuit boards: the most manufacturing-intensive parts of the building. |
| 4 | [[components/network\|Network]] | Switches, routers and fiber that connect servers to each other and to the outside world. |
| 6 | [[components/materials-and-minerals\|Materials and minerals]] | What the hardware and building are physically made of, and where those materials come from. |
| 6 | [[components/end-of-life\|End of life]] | What happens to servers, batteries and the building when they are retired. |
