A data center looks like one quiet building. Behind it sit a power grid, a water supply, a mine, a county zoning board, a chip fab and a neighborhood. This garden traces those connections, one note at a time.

> [!info] This garden is a work in progress
> Notes are marked 🌱 **seedling** (drafted, not yet checked), 🌿 **budding** (checked by a person) or 🌳 **evergreen** (stable, well sourced). Expect gaps and open questions. That is the point of a garden.

## Three ways in

1. **Start from a part of the building.** Open the [building explorer](static/explorer.htm), click a part, and follow it to its note. Or browse [[components/index|Components]].
2. **Start from an impact.** Pick a branch such as [[branches/environment|Environment]] or [[branches/communities|Communities]] and see which parts of the building drive it. All eight are in [[branches/index|Branches]].
3. **Start from a feedback loop.** See how effects reinforce or balance each other in [[loops/index|System dynamics loops]].
