---
title: "System dynamics loops"
description: "Feedback loops that connect the data center's parts and impacts."
---

These four loops are first drafts. The [[project/roadmap|roadmap]] lists eight more candidates (grid strain, energy bills, siting migration, critical mineral squeeze, circularity, heat reuse, disclosure pressure, hype and capex).

| Loop | Type | In one line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[loops/efficiency-rebound\|Efficiency rebound]] | R (reinforcing) | Efficiency gains make each unit of computing cheaper, which can raise total demand enough to grow total energy use. |
| [[loops/refresh-trade-off\|Refresh trade-off]] | Balancing, with a hidden cost | Replacing servers sooner lowers energy per task, but every new server carries manufacturing emissions and creates e-waste. |
| [[loops/water-vs-energy\|Water vs energy]] | Trade-off (balancing) | Evaporative cooling saves electricity but uses water on site; dry cooling saves water but uses more electricity, which uses water at power plants. |
| [[loops/community-pushback\|Community pushback]] | B (balancing) | Local impacts build opposition, which can lead to delays, stricter permits or moratoriums. Projects may then move elsewhere, shifting impacts rather than removing them. |
