[Project MACC 2027](https://mit-orcd.github.io/macc2027/) awards prizes for the best uses of agentic coding to advance research and education at MIT. Finalists are judged on an open-source codebase, a short write-up (including how humans and agents worked together) and a live demo at the January showcase.

| What MACC asks for | How we answer it |
| --- | --- |
| Open-source software the team created | This repo: the garden, explorer and models |
| Write-up including human–agent collaboration | The [[project/human-agent-log\|collaboration log]] and provenance fields on every note |
| Contribution to research or education | A shared teaching object across schools; an evidence base for our own research |
| Technical capability, innovation, originality | Interactive system dynamics inside a digital garden, built and maintained with agents |
| Robustness and reproducibility | Plain-text notes, sources on every number, CI builds, tested models |

## Areas we could enter

One entry can compete in several areas. Strong fits: **Engineering**, **MIT Education**, and **Architecture and Planning**. Possible: **Management** (economics and disclosure).
